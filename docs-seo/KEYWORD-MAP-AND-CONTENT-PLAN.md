# Keyword Map & Content Plan

> **Search volume, keyword difficulty and competition levels were NOT measured.** No keyword tool was available for this work, so none are shown below. To check demand, use Google Search Console (after a few weeks of data), Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) or Google Trends (region: Bangladesh).

Rule: one primary keyword per page, so pages don't compete with each other.

## Content plan (future blog posts)

The site is plain static HTML and has no blog yet. To add one, create a `blog/` folder with one HTML page per article (copy the layout from an existing page), link to it from the footer, and add each article to `sitemap.xml`. **Publish only finished articles, never empty placeholders.**

Suggested first 10 articles, in priority order:

1. How to Start Dropshipping in Bangladesh: A Step-by-Step Beginner Guide → links to dropshipping.html
2. Facebook Page vs Website: Where Should a New Online Seller in Bangladesh Start? → dropshipping.html
3. Online Earning for University Students in Bangladesh: Realistic Options (and Scams to Avoid) → opportunities.html
4. How to Do Product Research for an Online Store in Bangladesh → course-modules.html
5. Handling Cash on Delivery: Reducing Returns and Fake Orders → dropshipping.html
6. Simple Market Research Before You Invest Your Capital → business-support.html
7. How to Write a One-Page Business Plan → business-support.html
8. Testing a Business Idea With Little Money → business-support.html
9. Personal Branding for Freelancers: Optimising Your Facebook and LinkedIn → personal-branding.html
10. 12-Week Online Business Roadmap: What to Do Each Week → programs.html

For each article: one primary keyword in the title and H1, an original explanation with real local examples, 2–3 internal links to the pages above, and a WhatsApp CTA at the end. A Bangla version of the best-performing articles could reach many more searchers. Add those later as separate pages, linked with `hreflang`.
