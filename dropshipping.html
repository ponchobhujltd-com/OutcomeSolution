What is dropshipping?

Sell online without keeping stock

Dropshipping is a business model where you sell products online and a supplier ships them directly to your customer. You focus on finding the right products, building your store and marketing, without the cost of holding inventory.

In the Outcome Solution dropshipping track, you learn each part of the process and implement it step by step with guidance.

Dropshipping in Bangladesh: what is different

Most dropshipping guides are written for the US or Europe. Selling online in Bangladesh works differently, and our course is built around the local reality:

  • Many customers find and buy from sellers through Facebook pages and groups
  • Cash on delivery is common, so you need a plan for confirmations and returns
  • Delivery is usually handled through local courier services
  • Sourcing can be local (wholesale markets and resellers) or international
  • Trust matters: clear policies, real photos and fast replies make a difference

Honest expectations

Dropshipping costs less to start than stocking inventory, but it still needs a budget for marketing and tools, and time to learn. Profit is not guaranteed. Depending on how you operate, you may need a trade licence or other registrations; check current rules before you scale.

Dropshipping course FAQ

How long does the dropshipping course take?

Dropshipping and e-commerce are covered in month 2 (weeks 5–8) of the 3-Month Outcome Program. Month 1 builds the foundations you need first. See the course modules.

Do I need a website to start dropshipping?

Not necessarily. Many sellers in Bangladesh start with a Facebook page or shop, and later move to their own website. The course covers both options.

Can a university student do dropshipping?

Yes, if you can give it a few consistent hours each week. Product research, content and customer messaging can all be done from home with a laptop or smartphone.

How much money do I need to start?

It depends on your niche, products and marketing plan. Dropshipping avoids bulk stock costs, but you should budget for ads, tools and test orders. We help you plan this during the course.
