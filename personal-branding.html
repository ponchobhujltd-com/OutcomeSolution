Personal Branding

Build an online presence that earns trust and attracts opportunities.

Why it matters

People work with people they trust

Your personal brand is how people see you online. A clear, professional brand helps you win clients, sell products, find partners and stand out in a crowded market.

In our personal branding track, you define your story, build your profiles and create content consistently.

“Your personal brand is what people say about you when you are not in the room.”

What you will learn

Brand identity

Define your niche, values, message and visual style.

Profile optimisation

Make your Facebook, LinkedIn and other profiles professional.

Content creation

Plan and create posts, reels and visuals that connect.

Storytelling

Share your journey in a way that builds trust.

Portfolio building

Show your skills and results to clients and partners.

Audience growth

Grow your network and engagement consistently.

By the end of the track you will have

  • A clear personal brand statement
  • Optimised social media profiles
  • A two-week content plan and published posts
  • A simple portfolio of your work
  • A plan to keep growing your brand

Personal branding + dropshipping

A trusted personal brand makes selling easier. Our program connects both tracks so your brand supports your business and your business strengthens your brand.
